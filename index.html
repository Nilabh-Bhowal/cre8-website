CRE8 ROBOTICS

Welcome to the official website of Cre8 Robotics, FTC Team #24438! We are a passionate group of students located in the Des Moines area, dedicated to designing, building, and programming robots to compete in the FIRST Tech Challenge. Our team is committed to fostering creativity, teamwork, and innovation through robotics. Explore our site to learn more about our team, our projects, and how you can get involved!
