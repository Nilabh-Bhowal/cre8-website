FIRST Tech Challenge

FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) is a robotics competition for students in grades 7-12. Teams design, build, and program robots to compete in a game that changes every year. FTC emphasizes teamwork, creativity, and problem-solving skills, providing students with hands-on experience in STEM fields.

Learn More

Team History

The team started out as a FIRST Lego League team in 2019 called Cre8 The Future. After many successful years in the program, the team transitioned to FIRST Tech Challenge in 2023, picking up new members along the way. In 2026 the team decided to rebrand to Cre8 Robotics to better align with the program.
